Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans at any time? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, you're billed the prorated difference immediately. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the next billing cycle.
What counts as a "submission"? +
A submission is when a website visitor completes your estimator and submits their contact info. Partial completions (drop-offs) are tracked for analytics but don't count toward your limit.
Do you store my customers' data? +
Yes — leads and their answers are stored securely on our servers and are accessible only to your account. You can export or permanently delete all data at any time to comply with GDPR or CCPA requests.
Is there a long-term contract? +
No. All plans are month-to-month by default. Annual plans are prepaid for 12 months at a 20% discount and are non-refundable after the 30-day guarantee period.
Can I embed on multiple websites? +
Yes. The same estimator widget can be embedded on as many pages or domains as you like at no additional cost. Each estimator has a unique embed code.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) via Stripe. All transactions are processed securely — we never store your card details.
